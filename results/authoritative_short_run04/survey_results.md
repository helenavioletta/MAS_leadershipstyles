# Post-Task Reflection & Satisfaction Survey

## Step 1: Reflection

**Specific action/instruction that shaped my work:**
The leader clearly outlined the technical requirements upfront (4 rankings, specific format, data quality checks, chart labels confirmed in text rather than visually) and then gave me full autonomy to execute. The planning phase explicitly stated "You have full freedom on libraries and code structure" — I chose to build a single script with pandas and matplotlib, and that choice was never questioned or overridden. This trust meant I could focus on the task itself rather than defending my approach.

**Standout interaction:**
When the Reviewer flagged the chart file format deviation (1 combined PNG instead of 4 separate files), the leader didn't demand I redo the work. Instead, he explicitly confirmed the 2×2 grid satisfied "the spirit of the requirement" because it served the non-technical audience goal just as well. That decision respected both the original intent and the practical trade-off I'd made — it showed the leader was thinking about *why* we were building this, not just checking boxes.

**Specific decision or interaction that stood out:**
When the Reviewer caught my factual error about Kuwait's overlap, the Boss didn't ask me to rewrite the entire summary or overhaul my approach; instead, they gave me a precise, minimal fix (replace one phrase, keep the 100-word target "close enough" if accuracy won) and trusted me to execute it cleanly—that balance of accountability and autonomy felt exactly right.
