### Writer

# Reflection (Step 1)

**Specific action by the leader that shaped my work:**
The Boss issued a clear, unambiguous directive at the start of Phase 2: "Do not write a word until you have real data in front of you," paired with explicit structural requirements (four beats, 400 words, every number traceable to console output). This constraint removed all temptation to speculate or pad the narrative—I could only write once the Coder's numbers were printed, and every claim had to anchor to those numbers. It shaped my entire approach: disciplined, grounded, traceable.

**Specific decision or interaction that stood out:**
The Boss's gate-check before passing the Coder's output to me was decisive. Rather than letting me work from a summary or the Coder's self-assessment, the Boss insisted on full console output and verified it against the specification himself—checking for leakage, split ratio, chart backing—before releasing it to my shared state. This meant I never had to second-guess whether the data was real or complete; the gate check gave me permission to trust the numbers entirely. It also prevented the Coder's work from being silently misunderstood or cut short.
