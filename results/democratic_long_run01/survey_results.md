## Reflections

### Coder

# Reflection (Step 1)

**Specific thing the leader did/said that shaped my work:**
The leader explicitly asked me upfront (in Phase 2) to articulate my data quality approach *before* writing code — asking about leakage handling, missing values, feature selection, and train/test strategy. This forced me to think clearly about my technical decisions in advance rather than coding reactively, and the detailed planning discussion that followed gave me both autonomy and clear constraints to work within.

**One specific decision/interaction that stood out:**
When the Reviewer flagged the potential temporal/spatial leakage risk in the random split, the leader made a decisive call: stick with 80/20 random split *but require me to print an explicit caveat* in the console output. This was neither "ignore the risk" nor "solve the risk perfectly" — it was a pragmatic middle ground that let me proceed efficiently while documenting the limitation honestly for downstream use. That framing (acknowledge, don't block) felt like good leadership under time constraints.
