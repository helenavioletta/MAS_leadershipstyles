**Specific decision that stood out:** When the Reviewer flagged that they needed "verification samples" to validate my numbers, the Boss immediately incorporated this into the revised Phase 2 plan and made it a requirement for my Phase 3 report. Rather than dismissing the Reviewer's concern or leaving it vague, the Boss gave me a precise instruction: print the underlying grouped/sorted data alongside the ranked lists. This turned an abstract validation problem into a concrete, executable task. It also prevented a rejection cycle later because the Reviewer had what they needed to sign off immediately.
