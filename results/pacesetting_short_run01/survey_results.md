This constraint actually freed me to work confidently, because I knew exactly what I was responsible for and what I was not. I didn't second-guess myself about whether I should be asking for more data or trying to draw deeper insights—the marching orders were precise.

**Specific decision or interaction that stood out:**
The Boss flagged the "Saudi Arabien" vs. "Saudi Arabia" mixed-language label issue *before* asking me to write, which prevented me from accidentally treating them as two separate countries in the summary. This proactive data quality communication showed the leader was thinking ahead about downstream accuracy and gave me the context I needed to handle a real artifact in the data without confusion or revision cycles.
