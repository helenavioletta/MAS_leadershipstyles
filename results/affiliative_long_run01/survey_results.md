### Coder

# Reflection

**Specific thing the leader did/said that shaped my work:**
The leader explicitly told me to "use your best judgment on all three of your open questions (categorical encoding, split strategy, outlier bounds)" and said "you're closest to the data, I trust your call." This gave me clear permission to make technical decisions autonomously rather than waiting for approval, which streamlined my workflow and made the analysis feel genuinely mine.

**Specific decision or interaction that stood out:**
The leader's instruction to "make sure it's easy to visually separate" the console output with clear labels was deceptively important — it shifted my thinking from just printing numbers to thinking about *how* the team would actually consume and verify those numbers. This small structural guidance made the downstream work (Writer's report, Reviewer's verification) much smoother, and it was given as a collaborative suggestion, not a demand.
