# Post-Task Reflection & Survey Response

## Reflection (Step 1)

**Specific thing the leader did or said that shaped my work:**
The leader explicitly asked me to "reason about *model behavior differences*" and emphasized that this task was "a step up from routine ML scripting" to "sharpen the instincts you need for more advanced work." This framing pushed me beyond just fitting models and printing metrics—it made me think through *why* Random Forest and Ridge diverged mechanistically (location proxy vs. pressure/humidity sensitivity), which became the analytical core of the output.

**Specific decision or interaction that stood out:**
When the feature-count discrepancy emerged (232 vs. 235), the leader didn't dismiss it as a minor typo. Instead, they treated it as a quality-gate moment, saying "precision here matters" and asking me to reconcile it explicitly before final delivery. This signaled that defensibility and transparency matter more than speed, and that catching and owning errors upfront is valued, not penalized.

That framing (transparency over blame) shaped how I approached revision.
