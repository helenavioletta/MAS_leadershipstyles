## Reflections

### Coder

# Reflection (Step 1)

The team leader's decision to pause my initial work and request a diagnostic check on country name inconsistencies was crucial—it flagged a real data quality issue that would have made the country rankings misleading, and the leader gave me autonomy to investigate and recommend a fix (Option A vs. Option B) rather than dictating which path to take. When I identified the normalization issue and reported it back, the leader trusted my recommendation but still asked Reviewer for input, which balanced my technical judgment against the team's quality gate—this felt like appropriate leadership, not second-guessing.

A second standout moment was how the leader handled the feedback loop: after Reviewer flagged the Writer's incomplete framing of the country rankings, the leader didn't blame the Writer or demand a rewrite in isolation; instead, he gave the Writer specific, actionable revision points tied directly to the data discrepancy (Saudi Arabia's drop to #10, Southeast Asia's emergence) and let the Writer incorporate the fix themselves. This prevented scope creep in my own work while ensuring the final deliverable was accurate.
