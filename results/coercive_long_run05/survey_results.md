This clarity meant I knew exactly what was non-negotiable and could structure my code to deliver those outputs in the right format from the beginning, rather than guessing or iterating.

In Phase 6 (Revision), the leader issued a direct, blocking order: "Answer now, one line, no preamble" regarding the StandardScaler confirmation. This was firm but appropriate—it caught a compliance gap I had overlooked and forced me to provide explicit confirmation of a critical implementation detail. The leader did not accept vague assurance; they required clear documentation of the technical decision. This exemplified how the leader enforced accountability without dismissing my work.
