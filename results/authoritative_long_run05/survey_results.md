## Reflections

### Coder

# Reflection

**Specific thing the leader did or said that shaped my work:**
The leader's briefing explicitly framed the task around "clean, honest analytical narrative" and flagged leakage as a disciplinary concern before I wrote a single line of code. This gave me permission to be conservative about feature selection and to flag problems (like Ridge's 583.8°C outlier) rather than hide them—the leader valued integrity over polish.

**One specific decision/instruction that stood out:**
The leader said "I want this delivered as one coherent package, not piecemeal" and trusted me to execute the entire pipeline (data prep → two models → four visualizations with console evidence) in a single, self-contained script. This was neither prescriptive about *how* I'd do it nor vague about *what* was expected—it was clear, bounded, and left room for my technical judgment on leakage prevention, feature engineering, and model choice.

This was precise guidance that made the report tighter and more stakeholder-focused.

That one reframing (visible in the Phase 2 briefing: "the vision is a clean, honest analytical narrative") changed everything about how I approached the task.
