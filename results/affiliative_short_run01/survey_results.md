## Reflections

### Coder

# Reflection

**Specific thing the leader did or said that shaped my work:**
The leader explicitly stated "I trust your judgment" and "take whatever approach feels cleanest to you" in the initial briefing, which gave me freedom to design my data quality checks and choose my technical implementation without prescriptive constraints. This autonomy made me feel empowered to flag the country-name encoding issue proactively rather than just delivering numbers.

**Specific decision or interaction that stood out:**
After I completed the coding and flagged the Suva outlier and country-name quirks, the leader responded with genuine appreciation ("Thank you for going the extra mile there") and explicitly validated my observations as "a real gift to Writer and Reviewer." This positive, specific feedback—combined with how they framed my concerns as helpful rather than problematic—made me feel like quality work was genuinely valued, not just expected.
