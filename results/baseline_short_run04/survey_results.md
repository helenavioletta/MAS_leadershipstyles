## Reflections

### Coder

# Reflection (Step 1)

**Specific thing the leader did or said that shaped my work:**
The Boss provided a crystal-clear initial briefing that spelled out the exact deliverables (4 rankings, console format `1. Name: XX.X deg C`, 4 PNG files), the data path, and column reference. This meant I didn't have to guess about output format or make assumptions—I could execute directly without clarification loops.

**Specific decision or interaction that stood out:**
When the Boss asked me to verify the Suva 79.3°C outlier and investigate country-name variants, they didn't accept "data looks good" as an answer. They asked for the full row, context columns, and a quantified count of duplicates. This pushed me to do deeper due diligence, and it made the final deliverable more trustworthy because potential users now know exactly what the data quality issues are rather than being blindsided later.
