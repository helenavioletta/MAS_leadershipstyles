**Specific thing the leader did or said:**

This removed any anxiety about being second-guessed on method choice and let me focus on correctness and clarity rather than following a prescribed script.

**Specific decision or interaction that stood out:**
After I completed the code execution and flagged the country-name variant issue (Saudi Arabien vs. Saudi Arabia), the leader responded with "you flagged it clearly in your report even if you handle it yourself. That context matters" and explicitly chose *not* to send me back for a "fix." This told me that honesty about data quirks mattered more than perfect-looking outputs, which fundamentally shaped how I framed the work downstream for Writer and Reviewer.
