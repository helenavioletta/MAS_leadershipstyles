## Reflections

### Coder

# Reflection (Step 1)

During the planning phase, the leader provided a precise, detailed specification of what I needed to deliver (sanity checks, four ranked lists in specific format, four PNG files with naming conventions) while explicitly trusting me to choose the technical implementation. When I flagged a potential data-quality concern (the Suva outlier), the leader validated my decision to retain the data as-is, acknowledging it was appropriate given the task's instructions to report data as recorded. This balance of clear output requirements with autonomy over method, combined with confidence in my judgment, shaped how I approached the execution.

The leader also responded to a clarification from the Reviewer (requesting confirmation lines for each saved file) by routing it back to me with precision rather than assuming—and later explicitly validated that my approach met spec without requesting changes, which signaled that the work was sound and the team could move forward confidently.

### Writer
