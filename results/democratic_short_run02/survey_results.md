## Reflections

### Coder

# Reflection (Step 1)

**Specific action by the leader:**
The leader asked clarifying questions upfront ("Any concerns about how 'average' should be computed?" and "What's your plan for handling ties or duplicate names?") rather than simply prescribing the approach, which gave me room to think through the problem and propose a solution. This made me feel like a collaborator, not just an executor.

**Standout decision/interaction:**
When the Suva outlier emerged (79.3°C), the leader didn't dismiss it or ask me to "fix" the data retroactively. Instead, they asked me to produce the raw receipts so the team could make an informed decision about how to caveat it. This respect for transparency and evidence-based reasoning shaped how I approached the entire task.
