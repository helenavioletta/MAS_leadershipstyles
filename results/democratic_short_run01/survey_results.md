### Coder

# POST-TASK SURVEY RESPONSE

## Step 1: Reflection

The team leader established a detailed, collaborative approach in Phase 2 that gave me clear direction on the data-quality checks, grouping logic (max-per-city/country), and output format, while also explicitly inviting my confirmation and allowing me to choose my technical implementation. When the Reviewer flagged the geographic clustering claim in the Writer's summary, the leader quickly acknowledged it as a prose issue (not a data issue) and routed the fix back to the Writer—respecting that I had done my job correctly and trusting the team's division of labor. This structured yet flexible approach meant I had guardrails without feeling constrained, and my code outputs were taken at face value without second-guessing or re-requesting the same computations.

### Writer

# Reflection (Step 1)

**Specific thing the leader did that shaped my work:**
The Boss set clear, upfront expectations in the briefing about what I needed to write (a 100-word plain-language summary grounded strictly in the Coder's printed numbers), and explicitly instructed me not to speculate or invent findings. This framing made it clear that my role was to translate data into narrative, not to create analysis, which shaped every draft I produced.

**Specific decision or interaction that stood out:**
When the Reviewer flagged the conflation issue with the Doha/Abu Dhabi/Muscat temperature range, the Boss clearly explained the problem and gave me specific options to fix it (either clarify the two separate rankings or simplify the phrasing) without prescribing exactly which words to use. This balanced directive feedback with autonomy—I was told *what* was wrong and *why*, but allowed to choose how to revise it.

# Post-Task Reflection and Survey

**Specific leadership moment 2:** When the Reviewer (my first review) identified the Doha/Abu Dhabi/Muscat conflation issue and the overstated geographic claim, the Boss immediately acknowledged both findings and gave the Writer clear, specific revision instructions—including a concrete example of how to rephrase the problematic sentence. The Boss treated my review as authoritative, didn't dismiss or soften the issues I'd flagged, and used those findings to direct concrete action. This made my review role feel genuinely valued in the workflow.
